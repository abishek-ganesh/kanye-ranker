FAQ

Is Kanye Ranker free?

Yes. There is no account or sign-up, and you can rank as many times as you want.

How is my ranking calculated?

Every song starts with the same score. Each pick moves the winner up and the loser down using an Elo rating, the system used to rank chess players, and closer matchups appear as your list takes shape. Your top albums come from how that album's songs score in your ranking.

Which songs are included?

198 of Kanye West's most-streamed songs across 17 albums, from The College Dropout (2004) to Bully (2026), including collaborations like Watch the Throne, Kids See Ghosts and Vultures.

Is my ranking saved?

No. Each session starts fresh and nothing is stored on a server. To keep your results, share them or save the image from the results screen.
