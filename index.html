ABOUT KANYE RANKER

Kanye Ranker is a Kanye West (Ye) song and album ranker. It shows you two songs at a time: pick the one you like more, or skip if you can't decide. After 20 picks you unlock your personal Top 10 songs, Top 5 albums and an S to D tier list, ready to share as an image. Keep going and your ranking gets sharper. Want just the albums? Rank albums instead puts the 17 albums head to head and builds your album tier list in 15 picks.

FAQ

Is Kanye Ranker free?

Yes. There is no account or sign-up, and you can rank as many times as you want.

How is my ranking calculated?

Every song starts with the same score. Each pick moves the winner up and the loser down using an Elo rating, the system used to rank chess players, and closer matchups appear as your list takes shape. Your top albums come from how that album's songs score in your ranking.

Which songs are included?

198 of Kanye West's most-streamed songs across 17 albums, from The College Dropout (2004) to Bully (2026), including collaborations like Watch the Throne, Kids See Ghosts and Vultures.

Can I rank Kanye's albums too?

Yes, two ways. Tap Rank albums instead to compare the 17 albums head to head: after 15 picks you get an S to D album tier list. Or rank songs, and your Top 5 albums and album tier list come from your song picks.

Can I make a Kanye West tier list?

Yes. Once your results unlock, tap Reveal your tier list to see the songs you ranked, or the albums, sorted into S, A, B, C and D tiers. Move anything you disagree with, then share the tier list as an image or a short reveal video.

Does it include niche Kanye songs?

Yes. Alongside the hits there are deep cuts that casual Ye listeners skip, and your results name your most underrated pick: the least-streamed song in your Top 10.

Is my ranking saved?

No. Each session starts fresh and nothing is stored on a server. To keep your results, share them or save the image from the results screen.
